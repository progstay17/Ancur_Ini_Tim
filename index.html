Who We Are

Bridging Academia
& Industry

⚠️ UNOFFICIAL — Made by Ancur Ini Tim

Alpha Innovation Technology (AIT) is an Indonesian-based AI services company specializing in high-quality data annotation and AI resource development — sitting at the intersection of cutting-edge academic research and real-world enterprise AI needs.

Through our exclusive partnership with the Faculty of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science (FAIDAS) at Universitas Pelita Harapan (UPH), we deliver world-class annotation services to global enterprises, including Fortune 500 technology companies.

We are proudly backed by Multipolar Technology (IDX: MLPT) — 50+ years of industry trust, 15+ global awards in 2025, and deep partnerships with Microsoft, Google, IBM, and more.

This page is an unofficial internal hub. All info here is real — the vibes are extra.

Become the leading AI data services partner for top-tier technology companies across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific.
